About us

Alessandro Mauro

Noxford Agency is Alessandro Mauro's agency. You talk to him from start to finish.

Before every call he asks you, in a questionnaire of about five minutes, what work you do, how you work today and what weighs on you.

On the call he starts from there: he shows you how your day works, where time gets lost and how to fix it. Your answers are only used to prepare the call and the proposal. Work starts from Italy, and we can work with you remotely, by video call and online.
