The hooks

The first seconds decide everything

People scrolling their phone decide at once whether to stop. Every video opens with a line that speaks to your customer. A few examples:

  • “The phone rings and your hands are full.”
  • “How many bookings do you lose when you can't answer?”
  • “Your customer wrote at 11 pm. Who answers?”
  • “Before: the paper diary. After: everything on the phone.”
